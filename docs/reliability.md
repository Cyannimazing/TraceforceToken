## Fresh acquisition and result lifetime

Every new scan acquires fresh bounded data. It does not reuse a saved report, deduplicate against a server history, or persist evidence/report/chat state on the server. The response contains normalized evidence and coverage for inspection. A visitor can explicitly save/download a historical report in their own browser; reopening it does not refresh the chain observations.

Original upstream RPC bodies are deliberately not archived by the public runtime. This is the chosen no-server-evidence design, not an unfinished archive release requirement. Consequently a report/snapshot is not a full raw-response replay package, an external integrity attestation, or a guarantee of provider completeness. Deliberately invoked operator verification tools can produce separate public-chain artifacts.

Migrations/legacy job tests remain for compatibility. New scans use no durable queue, automatic retry, job-ID retrieval, server report retention, or resumable completion. Disconnects, provider/model failures and deadlines can require a new scan.

## Current evidence controls

The collector validates Base chain ID, pins finalized start/end block identities, checks successful receipts and event inclusion, and rechecks the pinned end block before returning. Mixed/conflicting block or receipt identity fails rather than becoming a fabricated clean result. Exact raw token/wei amounts remain decimal strings and use bigint arithmetic.

The analyzer normalizes fork-specific IDs, orders/deduplicates evidence deterministically, and produces versioned findings with rule parameters, cited records, caveats, and explicit coverage. Wallet net flow is not a balance. Funding/transfer links describe observed relationships, not ownership. A separate holder snapshot records exact balanceOf reads at the pinned finalized block for discovered candidates. It does not establish a complete historical holder census or live-current balances. Full token audits, internal traces, and broad DEX/router reconstruction remain unimplemented.

Holder candidates combine observed participants with GoPlus's documented Top10 list by default, or bounded Blockscout PRO pages when a key is configured. GoPlus's reported holder count and nine contract checks remain separate source context; ten provider rows are not an application wallet cap or a holder census. Discovery makes no balance calls: candidates are available to the funding phase before pinned balance verification begins. The later balance pass reuses discovery, requires the same token, finalized block and total supply, and preserves a recognized throttle from an attempted index page even if the primary RPC subsequently works. Discovery deferred solely by an earlier RPC throttle does not permanently block balance reads after verified RPC recovery. Failed/unreached balance checks remain null; percentages use exact total supply at the same block and never inferred float. The graph can display a paginated subset without excluding other retained candidates from analysis. External market metrics come from one exact matching Base/base-token pair selected by greatest valid reported liquidity, with deterministic tie-breaking. These are provider-reported context, not receipt-backed trading evidence; missing fields remain null.

Pool authentication is separate from trade classification. V2 pool candidates are checked before funding against the documented factory and reverse pair lookup, preventing recognized pools from grouping ordinary buyers. V3 checks also preserve supported pool identities even when a trade route is unclassified. Authenticated pools/hubs are excluded from ordinary wallet groups and early accumulation. At most 50 distinct pool addresses are attempted; failed authentication or a limit leaves identity unknown and disclosed.
